ADD A PAIR TO JOSHUA’S COLLECTION

The easy way — from your phone

1. Open the ADD NEW PAIRS folder in Google Drive. Create one folder per pair. Any recognizable name is fine, or use Pair 001, Pair 002, etc.
2. Upload one clear side photo of the whole shoe and one close-up of the box label or inside size tag. Keep the style code readable. No editing or background removal needed.
3. Add any extra angles you have. Optional: a short note about why you bought it, where you wore it, or why it matters to you.
4. Tell Cal “New pairs are uploaded.” Uploading does not publish them automatically.

For the best rotating view

Use the same shoe, camera height, lighting and plain background. Window light is ideal. Use your phone’s normal 1× camera, not Portrait mode. Keep the full shoe in frame with room around it.

Take eight photos moving around the shoe: outer side, front outer corner, front, front inner corner, inner side, back inner corner, back, back outer corner. Keep them in this order if convenient. Add top, sole and detail photos separately. A small turntable makes this faster, but is optional.

You do not have to take all eight to get started. Cal can match the exact model, colorway, edition and style code to verified real product photos. Your own photos are needed when condition, customization, wear or unique details matter. A single photo cannot show unseen sides accurately; we will never invent those views or imply stock photos show your exact physical pair.

What Cal checks before adding it

• Exact model, colorway and style code; uncertain identification stays in review.
• Existing catalog matches, including duplicates. Tell Cal if you own a second physical pair of the same model.
• Real-photo provenance, clear angles, cropping, lighting and display quality.
• Correct name, filters, collection count, wheel and viewer, plus phone and desktop layout.

Your upload stays separate from the public website until reviewed. Original photos stay unchanged. Display copies are resized and prepared for the site, with private label photos excluded from publication. Blank style codes stay blank until confirmed.

Status flow: Uploaded → Needs identification (only if needed) → Ready for preview → Published.

— Cal
