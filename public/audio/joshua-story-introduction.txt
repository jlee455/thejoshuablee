For a long time, I measured success by what I built. The companies. The growth. The next thing on the horizon. But there was a question I couldn't get away from: would my kids actually know me, or would they just know what I built? That changed the work. It changed my life. I stopped trying to keep business, family, and health in separate boxes. Together with Rachel, I started building a life that felt more integrated. What you read here is the story behind that shift, and why being human comes first in everything I do now.
