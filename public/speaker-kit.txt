JOSHUA B. LEE — SPEAKER INFORMATION

SHORT BIO
Joshua B. Lee is co-founder of StandOut Authority and Co-Founder & CVO of YOUmanize. He works with founders and executives on human-first authority, LinkedIn, and the way people discover and trust expertise in an AI-shaped world.

EXTENDED BIO
Joshua B. Lee is an entrepreneur, speaker, and author of Balance is Bullsh*t. Together with Rachel B. Lee, he co-founded StandOut Authority and YOUmanize. His work connects positioning, relationships, and public presence so leaders can communicate their expertise without losing their humanity. Joshua speaks on LinkedIn, trust, leadership, and the changing role of AI. He brings both his business experience and the personal lessons of rebuilding a more integrated life to his work with founders, executives, and teams.
