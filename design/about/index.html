THE MOMENTS THAT CHANGED THE PICTURE

Connect the dots.

01
A DIFFERENT DEFINITION

From monetizing to humanizing.

I built businesses around monetizing the web and managed more than $1 billion in advertising spend. The turning point was bringing that experience into work centered on human connection.

02
BECOMING DAD

The day the picture changed.

The day I was being honored with a jersey at an Austin Toros game, I also learned I was going to be a father. That changed what I wanted success to mean.

03
RACHEL

“Really? Buy your ticket.”

When Rachel told me she was moving to Seattle to work for Microsoft, I said I’d visit. She called my bluff. That conversation led to a promise that still shapes our life together: 100% us, 100% of the time.

04
2024 / A RETURN

Running beside my daughter.

After five years recovering from an Achilles injury and serious health complications, I ran again—beside my daughter. Health, family, and purpose came together in that moment.

WHAT COMES NEXT

I think of AI as a paintbrush. The person holding it brings the experience, intention, and imagination. That’s the spirit behind YOUmanize™: technology that helps your humanity reach further.
